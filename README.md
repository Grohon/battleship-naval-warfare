
# Battleship: Naval Warfare

A modern and elegant 2D Battleship game where you play against a computer AI. Built with React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, this project showcases a clean UI, smooth animations, and a challenging opponent.

## ✨ Features

- **Sleek, Modern UI:** A dark, naval-themed interface that's easy on the eyes.
- **Interactive Ship Placement:** Drag and drop your ships onto the board. Click or right-click to rotate them during the placement phase.
- **Smooth Animations:** Visual feedback for hits, misses, and sinking ships brings the game to life.
- **Smart AI Opponent:** The computer uses a "seek and hunt" algorithm, making it a formidable opponent.
- **Responsive Design:** Playable on both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Real-Time Fleet Status:** Keep track of which ships (both yours and the enemy's) are still afloat.

## 🚀 How to Play

1.  **Placement Phase:**
    -   You'll see your fleet of unplaced ships below the game boards.
    -   Drag each ship and drop it onto your grid ("Your Fleet").
    -   To rotate a ship, simply click or right-click on it (either in the unplaced ships area or after it has been placed on the board).
    -   You can also move ships that are already on the board by dragging them to a new valid location.

2.  **Battle Phase:**
    -   Once all ships are placed, the "Start Game" button will become active. Click it to begin the battle.
    -   Click on any cell in the "Enemy Waters" grid to fire a shot.
    -   A red explosion marker indicates a **hit**, and a grey marker indicates a **miss**.
    -   When all parts of a ship have been hit, it is **sunk**.

3.  **Winning the Game:**
    -   The first player to sink all five of the opponent's ships wins the game!
